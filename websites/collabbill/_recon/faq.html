Frequently asked questions

Is CollabBill free?

Yes. CollabBill is currently free to use — there is no paid tier and no trial. Opening the workspace takes a quick, free sign-in.

Do I need an account?

Yes — a quick, free sign-in (email or Google) gets you into the workspace. Your invoices and business data themselves live in your browser, not in the account.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local storage, on your current device — that copy is the one you always work on. When you are signed in, your workspace also syncs to a private cloud copy tied to your account (so it follows you to other devices). You can delete the cloud copy anytime from Settings → Cloud sync.

Can I back up or move my data?

Yes. Settings → Data lets you export a full JSON backup and import it again — for example to move to another browser or device, or to restore after clearing your browser data.

Does CollabBill sync between devices?

Yes, when you are signed in. Your workspace syncs automatically to a private cloud copy tied to your account, so signing in on another device brings your data with you. Signed out, everything stays local to that browser — and the JSON export/import backup always works.

Can I share an invoice with a client?

Yes. Any invoice can be shared through a public link — the client opens a clean, read-only view of that invoice (with a PDF download) without needing an account. You control the link: expire it, rotate it, or revoke it anytime.

Can I create recurring invoices?

Yes. Recurring schedules generate invoice drafts automatically on your schedule (monthly, quarterly, or custom) — always as drafts for your review, never auto-sent. Retainer agreements are tracked alongside, and payments on them are recorded manually like everything else.

Can I generate invoices as PDFs?

Yes. Every invoice, estimate, proposal, receipt, credit note, and debit note exports as a polished multi-page PDF, and can also be printed.

Can I use it for brand collaborations and sponsorships?

Yes — that is what CollabBill is designed for. Collaborations track campaigns, deliverables, deadlines, and payment milestones; invoices support usage rights, exclusivity, deposit/milestone/final billing, and credit or debit notes.

Can freelancers and agencies use it?

Yes. Freelancers get clients, estimates, invoices, and payment tracking. Agencies can manage client work per engagement with collaboration workspaces, expenses, and exports — though there are no team accounts or multi-user features today.

Does CollabBill send emails or payment reminders?

No. CollabBill never sends anything. Follow-up dates and statuses are manual tracking tools — you stay in control of every message.

Does CollabBill process payments?

No. CollabBill records payments that you have received through your own channels (bank transfer, UPI, PayPal, etc.) and tracks outstanding balances. It does not process or verify payments.

Do you support my currency?

CollabBill supports INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, AUD, CAD, SGD, JPY, and more (23 currencies in total), with per-currency formatting. Foreign-currency payments can be recorded with a manual exchange rate you enter.

Is CollabBill available internationally?

Yes — CollabBill is built for an international audience and works anywhere with a modern browser. Currencies, tax labels, and payment instructions are flexible (GST, VAT, or your own labels), though CollabBill does not guarantee compliance with any specific country’s tax or legal requirements.

Can I create GST invoices for brand deals?

Yes. Invoices support multi-tax including GST, VAT, and custom tax labels with separate rates per line, so Indian creators can bill brands with proper tax breakdowns. See the billing features on the Features page.

What happens to my data if I clear my browser?

Clearing browser data or using private browsing removes locally stored records — that is the trade-off of local-first storage. Export a full JSON backup from Settings → Data regularly and you can restore everything on any device.

Which devices and browsers does CollabBill work on?

Any modern browser on desktop, tablet, or phone — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. Nothing to install; the workspace and every PDF export run entirely in the browser.

Can I customize how my invoices look?

Yes — 15 document templates with adjustable accent colors and fonts, and every invoice, estimate, proposal, and receipt exports as a polished multi-page PDF you can print or send.

Is CollabBill a full accounting system?

No. CollabBill is a billing and business management tool for independent professionals. It tracks invoicing, payments, and expenses — it is not double-entry bookkeeping and does not replace an accountant.

How can I get support?

From inside the app: the Feedback page reaches the person who builds CollabBill directly — the fastest channel for bugs, questions, and feature requests. You can also write to support@collabbill.com.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Your local workspace: Settings → Data clears everything in your browser, and Settings → Cloud sync deletes your synced cloud copy. Your account: delete it from your Clerk account profile (the sign-in menu). For anything else, send an erasure request through the in-app Feedback page and it will be actioned.
